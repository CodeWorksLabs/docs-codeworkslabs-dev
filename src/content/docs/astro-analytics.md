`@codeworkslabs/astro-analytics` is an experimental private Astro integration with explicit provider configuration, environment-specific enablement, bounded event queueing, safe custom endpoints, and a Starlight wrapper.

It is not yet published to npm. Installation and production-use guidance will appear when the product reaches its public-alpha gate.
